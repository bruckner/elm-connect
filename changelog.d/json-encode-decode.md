- Breaking: `Proto.Json` is split by direction into
  `Proto.Json.Encode` and `Proto.Json.Decode`, as elm/json splits
  `Json.Encode` and `Json.Decode`, and `Proto.Json` is removed. Code
  generated by 0.1 stops at `import Proto.Json` (regenerate). Their
  signatures name elm/json's types qualified, `Json.Encode.Value` and
  `Json.Decode.Decoder`.
- Breaking: 0.1's decoders keep their names in `Proto.Json.Decode`
  (`enum`, `int64`, `timestamp`, `members` and the rest), apart from
  `Proto.Json.float`, the `double` decoder, which is
  `Proto.Json.Decode.double`; `Proto.Json.Decode.float` reads a `float`
  field, as `Proto.Binary.float` does.
- Breaking: 0.1's encoders move to `Proto.Json.Encode` under names that
  say what they give: `objectOfFields` is `object`, `repeatedField`
  `repeated`, `dictField` `dict`, `boolMapField` `boolMap`,
  `implicitField` `implicit`, `implicitFloatField` `implicitDouble`,
  `implicitBytesField` `implicitBytes`, and the value encoders `encodeInt64`,
  `encodeInt64AsInt`, `encodeFloat`, `encodeBytes`,
  `encodeBytesAsBytes` and `encodeEmpty` are `int64`, `int64AsInt`,
  `double`, `bytes`, `bytesAsBytes` and `empty`. docs/migrating.md,
  "The JSON codecs", has every old name beside its new one.
- Breaking: `Proto.Json.Decode.field` and `fieldEither` have the names
  and types of 0.1's `Proto.Json.field` and `fieldEither`, but leave
  the check that the JSON is an object to `Proto.Json.Decode.message`,
  as 0.1's did not: wrap a hand-written pipeline of them in `message`.
  `Proto.Json.Decode.any` takes the registry, where 0.1's
  `Proto.Json.any` took none.
