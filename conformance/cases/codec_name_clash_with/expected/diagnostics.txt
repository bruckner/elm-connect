  × The package `shelf.v1` has two definitions whose codecs both become the

  help: The JSON codecs of a message or oneof that holds a
        `google.protobuf.Any`, directly or through its fields, take the
        registry and are named with `With` at the end (`encodeNoteWith`), so
        the JSON codecs of a type named for it with `With` at the end
        (`NoteWith` beside `Note`) take the same names, and a module cannot
        define two values with one name. Rename one of them in the proto, or
        rename one with `rename=shelf.v1.BoxWith:<Elm name>`.
