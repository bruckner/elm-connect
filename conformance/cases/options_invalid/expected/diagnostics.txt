options::invalid_rename

  × The rename `notes.v1.Note.title` is not `<proto full name>:<Elm name>`.
  help: A rename names a proto element by its full name and gives its Elm
        name, the two separated by `:`, such as `notes.v1.Note:Memo`; neither
        may be empty.

options::invalid_module_prefix

  × The module prefix `api` is not an Elm module name.
  help: `module_prefix` is dot-separated Elm module segments, such as
