# googleapis files

`google/api/http.proto`, `google/api/annotations.proto` and
`google/api/field_behavior.proto` are copied unmodified from
[googleapis](https://github.com/googleapis/googleapis) at commit
`525d4c82dc1dd2fa3884198a8006a7bf5b48ac9d`. The generator embeds them so
that protos importing `google/api/annotations.proto` or
`google/api/field_behavior.proto` compile without a googleapis checkout.
